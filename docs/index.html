FAQ

Questions

Can this get me banned?

GuideStrap never touches the game while it runs. It only changes files and settings Roblox reads when it starts, like Bloxstrap and Fishstrap. It isn't an exploit and doesn't give any gameplay advantage. FastFlags are limited to the ones on Roblox's own allowlist.

Does it change my normal Roblox?

No. Everything happens in GuideStrap's own copy of Roblox. Opening Roblox without GuideStrap always gives you the original game, sounds and settings.

Will it make my PC lag?

GuideStrap barely uses anything while you play. If your PC lags, open Graphics and press "Load recommended", pick a preset like Smooth or Performance, and keep your FPS cap at your screen's refresh rate.

How does the server list work?

Right-click a game (or use the Server list button on the Play tab) to see its open servers with their ping and FPS, fastest first. Click Join on the one you want.

Why is my playtime empty?

Playtime is counted while GuideStrap stays in the tray. Turn on "Stay in the tray while Roblox is open" in Settings.

Do my friends need GuideStrap to join me?

No. If you turn on "Let friends join you" in the Discord tab, the Join button on your Discord status works for anyone with Roblox.

Where are my saved logins kept?

On your PC only, encrypted with Windows so they only work for your Windows user. They're never uploaded anywhere, and they're left out of backups.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings, then Uninstall. You can keep your settings for a reinstall. Your normal Roblox stays as it is.
